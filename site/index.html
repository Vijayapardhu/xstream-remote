Control your Android TV with precision & privacy

Features

Refined for every member of the home

Thoughtfully designed controls, tactile feedback, and smart context awareness come together for an experience that feels premium without sacrificing power.

Easy & larger controls

Bigger buttons, generous spacing, and larger text make navigation effortless for all ages without cluttering the interface.

Deeper haptics

Carefully tuned primitive-based thud, click, and tick patterns deliver a satisfyingly tactile response with every keypress.

App-aware controls

Context adapts automatically — Live TV shows Guide/Options/Captions, media apps reveal playback controls, and others surface quick actions.

Home-screen widgets

Keep essential controls close with sleek 2×2 and 4×2 widgets for Power, Back, Home, volume, mute, and playback.

Real app icons

Authentic artwork for YouTube, Netflix, Hotstar, Prime Video, Spotify, and the Play Store brings a polished look to your app tray.

LAN-only & private

Per-install RSA client certificates, TV certificate pinning, and local-only traffic mean your controls never leave your network.

Get started

Pair once, control forever

Secure pairing is built in. Each install generates its own client certificate, and it's pinned to your TV after a quick code verification.

1

Install

Build or install Xstream Remote on your Android phone from a local build.

2

Pair securely

Enable USB debugging on your TV and enter the six-character code shown on screen to pin the certificate.

3

Control anywhere on LAN

Use the remote, widgets, or app shortcuts over Wi-Fi. No account, no cloud, fully local.

Download

Build locally. Stay in control.

Xstream Remote is released source-only — no bundled client key, no signed APK in the repo. Build it yourself and sideload with confidence.

Download v2.0 APK (direct) View repository

Build with XSTREAM_STORE_PASS set (see docs/INSTALL.md) then sideload with adb install -r apk\XstreamRemote-2.0.apk See SETUP_BOX.md for details.

Privacy & safety

Built with privacy by default

No telemetry, no cloud dependencies, and strict scope of operation. Your TV remains under your control.

Passive capture only No ARP spoofing, no MITM, no credential extraction.
Safe boundaries No DRM or subscription bypass, no flashing, no unlocking, no factory reset, no package deletion.
Scoped access Phone ADB is in scope; TV USB debugging remains out of scope.
Encrypted & pinned TV certificate fingerprint pinned after pairing. All control traffic stays TLS over your LAN.
